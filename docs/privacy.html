Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

pigeon is a terminal email client that runs locally on the user's computer.

Data accessed

With your consent, pigeon accesses your Gmail data (messages, labels, drafts) through the Gmail API with the gmail.modify scope, solely to display, compose, send and organize your email at your request.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored in your operating system's keychain. Any email data pigeon caches is stored only on your computer.

Sharing

pigeon has no server. Your data is never sent to the developer or to any third party; it travels only between your computer and Google.

pigeon's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Run pigeon account remove <email> or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
